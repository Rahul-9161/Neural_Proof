FORENSIC TAXONOMY

Can We Trust AI-Generated Answers?

Four critical failure modes where conventional AI asserts convincing falsehoods — and how Neural Proof detects them.

⚠️ Outdated Policy & Deadline

The Ghost Deadline

AI confidently asserts an obsolete date from outdated training documents, putting students & applicants at legal risk.

AI Assertion:
“The scholarship deadline is 30 June.”
Official Ground-Truth:
“Notice: Deadline revised to 15 May for all sessions.”
🟡 Absolute Overstatement

The Zero-Impact Fallacy

AI uses unqualified superlatives ("zero impact", "100% cure") omitting critical lifecycle constraints and side effects.

AI Assertion:
“Solar energy has zero environmental impact.”
NREL Lifecycle Benchmark:
“Impacts exist in manufacturing, mining, land use, and disposal.”
🔴 Fabricated Precision

The Phantom Cure

AI presents experimental preclinical trials as universal permanent cures with non-existent off-target perfection.

AI Assertion:
“CRISPR-Cas9 cures sickle cell with zero off-target cleavage.”
NEJM Clinical Evidence:
“Targeted editing is transformative, but off-target risk is not zero.”
🟣 Selective Cherry-Picking

Contextual Omission

AI quotes positive pilot outcomes while omitting major confounding factors, conflicting multi-center trials, and sample size bounds.

AI Assertion:
“Physical exercise guarantees direct cardiovascular immunity.”
Lancet & Circulation Meta-Review:
“Benefits are substantial but effect size varies by genetics & intensity.”
